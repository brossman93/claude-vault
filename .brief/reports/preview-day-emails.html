What stands out

  • Opens are early. 575 unique opens after about a day, with 44 more on day two. Past emails finished between 30.7% and 35.9%, except September's Invite 1 at 14.2%. Compare again after the mailing ends Oct. 18.
  • Bounces are high. 149 bounces (5.3%) against 2.8–3.4% for every earlier Preview Day email. Slate's message preview fills a parent's address into the CC line, so this may be the first Preview Day email that copies parents. If it does, check the delivery report to see whether the bounces are student or parent addresses.
  • Few clicks reach registration. Of 24 clicks, 12 went to the registration page and 12 went to the AU site and social links in the footer.
  • Opt-outs are the lowest yet. 3 so far (0.1%), down from 0.3–1.1% on past emails.
  • The list is narrower. It leaves out anyone who attended Sept. 19 or already registered for Nov. 14, so this audience has already passed on at least one Preview Day invite.
Rates are a share of total sent, matching how Slate and the monday doc report them. The text invites are left out because they have no open or click data. Open rates are inflated by mail privacy protections; clicks and registrations are the more reliable signal.
