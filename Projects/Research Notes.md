# Research Notes

Brent's takeaways from higher ed reading, for AU comms.

- **Students decide before they inquire** (Higher Ed Insights, "Before the Funnel"): they research through social, AI, consultants and peers without leaving data. By the time they inquire or visit, they mostly want confirmation. Shift the [[UG Inquiry Sequence]] from awareness toward confirmation.
- **Visits come later** (AACRAO "Admit It"): many students visit after applying or being admitted, since apps are easier and visits cost more. Suggests a post-admission visit campaign. Follow-up questions to ask: was your visit different from others? What did you want covered that wasn't?
- **Admissions habits to break** (Enrollify Ep. 76): don't "check in"; ask a direct question ("Is something keeping you from completing your application that I could help with?"). Use personal or curious subject lines, not promotional ones. Post-visit follow-up should be a personal email or call, not a survey.
- **Blogging stats** (Orbit Media 2026): AI use doesn't correlate with better results. Underused tactics worth trying at AU: influencer and faculty collaborations, 2,000+ word articles, original student survey data, infographics, webinars (e.g., a free college planning workshop for students and parents).
- **Other ideas:** a post-non-enrollment survey ("Where did you go? Why?") with a gift-card drawing. A centralized enrollment dashboard and closer strategy work with admissions.
