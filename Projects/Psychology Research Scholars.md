# Psychology Research Scholars (PRS)

AU psychology program launch. Team: [[Kyle Hauenstein]], [[Suzanne Favri]], Brent.

PRS is essentially an honors track for psych majors (roughly 20–30% of them). Scholars join international research projects through the Psychological Science Accelerator.
