# HS Underclassmen Comms

How to talk to 9th and 10th grade inquiries, who aren't ready for the practical UG nurture.

- **Brent's proposal:** stay present with aspirational, experience-driven content (student life, events, Day in the Life video), timed to school-year moments, with ad retargeting throughout. Nine touchpoints from freshman fall to junior year, then into the [[UG Inquiry Sequence]].
- **Decision (9/22):** [[Kaitlynn Payne|KP]] said 1–2 emails, then the regular flow senior year.
- **Research:** Encoura 2026 (9th/10th graders) and RNL 2025 show this group leans toward peers, campus life and virtual tours, and has the least interest in cost and aid. Majors are still their #1 website topic.
- **Open questions for admissions:** when do underclassmen visit? How is CCP marketed, and how does it convert to UG?
