# Application Push

Fall 2026 effort to close the UG application gap.

## Context
- Apps down ~10% from last year, which was itself a record low (Peede, 9/30). Last year: 2,600 apps, 530 enrolled; marketing is responsible for ~200.

## Brent's highest-leverage proposals (presented 10/6)
1. Post-visit comms: 330 AC contacts have visited but not applied.
2. A 3–4 email series to CommonApp prospects ("finish your application").
3. New welcome emails for college fair inquiries and CommonApp suspects, then into the nurture.
4. Application-page and visit-page abandonment emails (need working site tracking).
- Longer term: a full-funnel dashboard (see [[Reporting]]).
