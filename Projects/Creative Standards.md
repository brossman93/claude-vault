# Creative Standards

The quality bar [[Jon Parrish Peede|Peede]] set for marketing on 9/30: a "culture of consistent excellence."

- Show undergrads as 17–21: no facial hair; students clearly students, faculty clearly faculty; balanced or female-leaning gender mix, especially on covers.
- Feature current faculty, not those who have left or retired. No outdated building shots.
- Visuals should tell the story alone. Active voice, not passive. Nothing that only makes sense with outside context.
- Promote major gifts (the Dwight gift was missed). Get more third-party coverage.
