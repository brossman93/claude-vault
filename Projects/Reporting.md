# Reporting

Comms reporting and the marketing scorecard.

## What to measure
- **Health:** delivery 95%+, hard bounce under 2%, spam complaints under 0.1%.
- **Engagement:** unique click rate (primary), click-to-open, open rate (directional), unsubscribes.
- **Outcomes:** visits and apps year over year; inquiry-to-visit, inquiry-to-app and visit-to-app rates; median time between each.
- Cut by lead score tier, audience and lead source.
