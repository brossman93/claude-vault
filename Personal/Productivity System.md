# Productivity System

Brent's own note on his setup, after worrying he was tweaking systems instead of working.

- **monday:** all AU projects and tasks.
- **Obsidian:** thinking and notes, work or personal; meeting tasks go to monday right after.
- **Saintmaker:** each morning, pick the top 3–5 tasks and circle the most important.
- **Apple Reminders:** everyday capture, groceries, and work tasks to enter in monday later.
- ClickUp cut, unless it could replace Reminders on the phone.

## Principles he leans on
- **Gall's Law:** a complex system that works evolves from a simple one that worked. Start simple.
- **Recursive self-improvement:** foundational improvements make room for finer ones (like James Clear's keystone habits).
- **Mailman Syndrome:** the drain of work that's never finished.
