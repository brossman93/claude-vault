# Faith and Ideas

Brent is Catholic and writes short idea notes on faith, philosophy and culture.

- **Marketing should educate:** most marketing appeals to instinct; good marketing is persuasive speech that respects reason. (Unfinished essay.)
- **On AI and tech:** skeptical of transhumanism and of Silicon Valley's "savior complex"; tools are morally neutral (Churchill, C.S. Lewis). Notes on *You Are Not Data* and Dario Amodei's "We Must Pace the Frontier."
- **Scripture:** James 1:6–8 and the psychology of the double-minded man. Aquinas's *Catena Aurea*.
- **Questions worked through:** whether smoking is a sin (damage vs. benefit, not a utilitarian test; Jimmy Akin says moderation is fine); the "Islamic dilemma" from GodLogic.
- **Story idea:** a non-believing paramedic called into a church during Mass, converted by its beauty; told from the inside, like the other side of *The Screwtape Letters*.
- **Fragments:** 33 as the age of coming of age (Aristotle, Jesus, hobbits).
